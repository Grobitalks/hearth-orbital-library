Hearth Orbital Library

1,500 public-domain stories, split into page files for reading inside a VRChat world. Non-commercial.

The texts come from Project Gutenberg, with thanks to its volunteers. The Project Gutenberg header, footer and licence were removed from these files, so they are not Project Gutenberg eBooks. All of them are in the public domain in the United States; check your local laws if you are elsewhere.

The book list is in catalog.json; the pages are in b/<id>/<n>.txt.
