The ace of spades, or Spadille, was always the first trump; the ace of clubs (Basto) always the third. Mrs. Manley, under Swift’s directions, wrote a <i>Narrative of Guiscard’s Examination</i>, and the incident greatly added to the security of Harley’s position, and to the strength of the Government.
