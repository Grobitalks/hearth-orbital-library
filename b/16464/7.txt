INDEX AND PRONUNCIATION OF THE MORE FREQUENTLY OCCURRING PLACE AND PERSONAL-NAMES.

It will simplify matters for the English reader if the following points respecting the pronunciation of proper names in medieval Irish, are borne in mind:

Each <i>simple</i> word is accented on the first syllable.

Pronounce:a (long), as in <i>aught</i>; a (short), as in <i>hot</i>.
c with slender vowels (e, i), as in <i>king</i>; never as <i>s</i>.
c with broad vowels (a, o, u), as in <i>car</i>; never as <i>s</i>.
ch with slender vowels (e, i), as in German <i>Ich</i>; never as in <i>church</i>.
ch with broad vowels (a, o, u), as in German <i>Buch</i>; never as in <i>church</i>.
d with slender vowels (e, i), as in French <i>dieu</i>.
d with broad vowels (a, o, u), as in <i>thy</i>.
e (long), as in <i>ale</i>; e (short), as in <i>bet</i>.
g with slender vowels (e, i), as in <i>give</i>; never as <i>j</i>.
g with broad vowels (a, o, u), as in <i>go</i>; never as <i>j</i>.
gh with slender vowels (e, i) is slender ch <i>voiced</i>.
gh with broad vowels (a, o, u) is broad ch <i>voiced</i>.
i (long), as in <i>feel</i>; i (short), as in <i>it</i>.
mh and bh intervocalic with slender vowels, as <i>v</i>.
mh and bh intervocalic with broad vowels, as <i>w</i>.
o (long), as in <i>note</i>; o (short), as in <i>done</i>.
s with slender vowels (e, i), as in <i>shine</i>; never as <i>z</i>.
s with broad vowels (a, o, u), as <i>s</i>.
t with slender vowels (e, i), as in <i>tin</i>.
t with broad vowels (a, o, u), as in <i>threw</i>.
th, like <i>h</i>.
u (long), as in <i>pool</i>; u (short), as in <i>full</i>.

The remaining consonants are pronounced almost as in English.
