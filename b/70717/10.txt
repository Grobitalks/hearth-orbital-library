Then listen Lordings, if ye list to weet iii

Yet has full large to liue, and spend at libertie.

But all his mind is set on mucky pelfe, iv

In vaine he feares that, which he cannot shonne: vii
  For who wotes not, that womans subtiltyes

Then is he not more mad (said <i>Paridell</i>) viii
