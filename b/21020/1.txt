[<i>Aloud.</i>] Sir, pray tell the worthy gambler Charudatta in my name that I shall pay him a visit this evening.

<i>Maitreya.</i> [<i>Aside.</i>] What else does she expect to get out of a visit to our house? [<i>Aloud.</i>] Madam, I will tell him--[<i>Aside</i> to have nothing more to do with this courtezan. [<i>Exit.</i>

<i>Vasantasena.</i> Take these jewels, girl. Let us go and bring cheer to Charudatta.
