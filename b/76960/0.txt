<i>Sub.</i> Friends, sprites, <i>et cetera</i>--We’ve assembled thus,
        The state of our dominions to discuss;
        Each day we read, in the <i>Subaqueous Times</i>,

        Daily committed with impunity,

        Till none are safe who roam the ocean o’er.

<i>Cor.</i> They’re just as safe as if they stayed on shore;
        There, too, I’m told, you’ll find on the increase--
        Burglaries--robberies--but <i>not</i> police.

<i>Sub.</i> Pooh! pooh! on land we’re quite out of our element;

        At all attempts to do so, he has laughed,

        All commerce doth he from the waters sweep--

        None cross the seas thro’ fear of this vile rover--
        Their spirits fail, ere they get half seas over--
        In short, unless mankind from him we save,
        There’ll soon be <i>no</i> life on the ocean wave.

<i>Sub.</i> Can’t catch him.

<i>Tempestia.</i> Raise a storm and wreck him.

<i>Sub.</i> Nay--his guilt’s plain.

<i>Serena.</i> Too plain for my denial.

<i>Sub.</i> Can you reclaim a tiger?

<i>Serena.</i> You can tame him!
        Let me attempt the task.

<i>Cor.</i> Nay, have a care,
        We don’t want Conrad made a tame affair.

<i>Sub.</i> Say by what potent magic will you try?<i>Serena.</i> One which the rudest breast can purify--
        Lifting the soul all meaner thoughts above--
        The magic influence of woman’s love.

<i>Cor.</i> Ho! ho! I see--the Corsair’s handsome face
        And figure, eh?

<i>Azurina.</i> A most decided case.

<i>Cor.</i> You’re smitten, miss.

<i>Serena.</i> I scorn the imputation;
        I am a fairy, and I know my station!

<i>Serena.</i> Madam, I’ve heard of fast young men on town,
        Desperate dogs, by marriage settled down--

        Smokers, I’ve heard, have put their pipes out--nay
        I’ve even heard of latch-keys thrown away.
        Can love do this, and yet be unavailing,
        To cure a paltry pirate’s little failing?
        Let Conrad only get a loving wife,        And, on my word, he’ll lead another life.

<i>Sub.</i> Enough--we’ll try your plan.

<i>Serena.</i> I ask no more,
        Than leave of absence for a month on shore--

        Then wreck him, or whate’er you please--
