Alfred Kreymborg, one of the foremost advocates of free-verse rhythmical drama, was born in New York City, 1883. PAUL GREENE

Paul Greene, one of the most promising of the University of North Carolina Playmakers, was born in 1894 on a farm near Lillington, North Carolina. Koch."The Aim of the Carolina Playmakers," says Professor Koch, "is to build up a genuinely native drama, a fresh expression of the folk-life in North Carolina, drawn from the rich background of local tradition and from the vigorous new life of the present day. Out of the many conflicts of American life, past and present, Mr. Greene sees possibilities for a great native drama. <i>White Dresses</i> presents a fundamental aspect of the race problem in America.

And you and Jim can live here as long as you please. Is that all right, Jim?JIM. [<i>Uncertainly.</i>] Yes-suh, yes-suh, Mr. Mawgin! An' I thanks you 'specially.

MORGAN. [<i>Going up to</i> AUNT CANDACE.] Mary and Jim are going to be married to-morrow, Candace. It'll be a lucky day for you. [<i>She makes no answer, but continues her trancelike stare in the fire.</i> MORGAN <i>comes to</i> MARY <i>and offers his hand. She fails to see it.</i>] Child, what I've had to do to-night has hurt me a whole lot worse'n you.... Good-night, Mary.

[<i>He stands a moment looking at the floor, then goes out quietly.</i>

JIM. [<i>Coming up to</i> MARY.] Miss Mary, don't look lak dat. I's gwine do better, I's.... [MARY <i>keeps her head muffled in her apron</i>.] Honey, I's sho' gwine make you a good man.

[MARY <i>pays no attention to him. In his embarrassment he strums his guitar, clears his throat, props his foot up on a chair rung, and begins singing in a low voice.</i>]

JIM. Lyin' in the jail house,
A-peepin' th'ough de bars....

AUNT CANDACE. [<i>Waking from her reverie.</i>] Bring me de li'l black box, gal. Bring me de box! [MARY <i>drops her apron and stares dully at the floor</i>.] Bring me de box! [<i>Half-screaming.</i>] Bring me de box, I say! [<i>Trembling and groaning, she stands up.</i> MARY <i>goes to the chest and brings her the black box</i>.
